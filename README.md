# E-commerce-Dashboard
1. Project Overview
The Madhav E-Commerce Sales Dashboard is an interactive business intelligence project developed using Microsoft Power BI to analyze and monitor the sales performance of an e-commerce business.

The project focuses on transforming raw sales data into meaningful business insights through interactive visualizations, key performance indicators (KPIs), data modeling, and analytical reporting.

The dashboard provides a comprehensive overview of business performance by analyzing sales revenue, product quantities, profitability, customer contributions, geographical sales distribution, and payment preferences.

It enables business stakeholders to understand sales trends, identify profitable product categories, evaluate customer purchasing patterns, and make informed business decisions.

🎯 2. Project Objectives
Analyze overall e-commerce sales performance.
Track total sales, quantity, profit, and AOV.
Identify top-performing states based on sales revenue.
Understand monthly profit trends and fluctuations.
Analyze category-wise product demand.
Identify customers contributing significantly to revenue.
Evaluate customer payment preferences.
Compare profitability across product sub-categories.
Enable interactive business analysis through quarterly and geographical filters.
Convert business data into actionable insights.

🧮 10. DAX Measures & Calculations
The following are representative DAX measures that can be used in the project. Column names and calculations should be aligned with the actual Power BI model.

Total Sales

dax
Total Sales =
SUM(Details[Amount])
Total Quantity

dax
Total Quantity =
SUM(Details[Quantity])
Total Profit

dax
Total Profit =
SUM(Details[Profit])
Total Orders

dax
Total Orders =
DISTINCTCOUNT(Orders[Order ID])
Average Order Value

dax
Average Order Value =
DIVIDE(
    [Total Sales],
    [Total Orders],
    0
)
Profit Margin

dax
Profit Margin =
DIVIDE(
    [Total Profit],
    [Total Sales],
    0
) * 100
Year-to-Date Sales

dax
YTD Sales =
TOTALYTD(
    [Total Sales],
    Orders[Order Date]
)
💡 11. Key Business Insights
Based on the dashboard:

Overall Sales: The business generated approximately 438K in total sales.
Total Quantity: Approximately 6K products were sold.
Profitability: Total profit reached approximately 37K.
Category Performance: Clothing dominates category-wise quantity contribution at 63%.
Payment Preferences: COD accounts for the largest payment share at 44%.
Geographical Performance: Maharashtra leads among the displayed states in sales.
Monthly Profitability: November and January are among the stronger positive-profit months.
Product Profitability: Printers are the highest-performing displayed sub-category.
Customer Analysis: The customer-wise chart highlights differences in individual revenue contribution.
These findings are based on the displayed dashboard values and visual comparisons, not an independent audit of the source dataset.
